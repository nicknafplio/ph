1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow a site to remember your preferences and, with consent, to gather information about how it is used.

2. Strictly necessary cookies

A small number of strictly necessary cookies are used to remember your cookie preferences. These do not require consent, and this site does not otherwise depend on cookies to function.

Necessary cookies persist until your preference is changed. Analytics cookies, where accepted, persist for the durations set out above, after which they expire automatically.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the site footer. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics tracking; it does not affect data already processed under a prior consent.
