Cookie Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Pigeon Hill Advisory Limited uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains more generally how we collect and use personal information.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, smartphone or other device when you visit a website.

They can be used for a variety of purposes, including enabling a website to function correctly, remembering choices you have made and helping website operators understand how their websites are used.

Some cookies are deleted when you close your browser, while others remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

2. Cookies we use

This website uses only a limited number of cookies.

We use:

  • strictly necessary cookies, which are required to remember your cookie preferences; and
  • analytics cookies, which are used only where you have chosen to accept them and help us understand how visitors use the website.

We do not use cookies for personalised advertising, remarketing or behavioural targeting.

3. Strictly necessary cookies

Strictly necessary cookies enable essential functions of the website.

In particular, we use a cookie or similar browser storage technology to remember the choice you make about analytics cookies. This prevents us from having to ask you to make the same choice every time you visit the website.

These technologies do not require your consent because they are necessary to record and respect your privacy choices.

PurposeDuration
Remembering your cookie preferencesUp to 12 months

The precise cookie or storage name may vary depending on the consent-management technology used by the website.

4. Google Analytics

If you choose to accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a website analytics service provided by Google.

Google Analytics helps us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use this website. For example, it may provide information about:

  • which pages are visited;
  • how visitors arrived at the website;
  • how long visitors spend on the site;
  • general interactions with the website;
  • browser and device type; and
  • approximate geographic location.

We use this information to understand the performance of the website and identify ways in which it can be improved.

Google Analytics is not used by Pigeon Hill Advisory for personalised advertising, remarketing or behavioural targeting.

Analytics cookies are not set until you have chosen to accept analytics cookies.

6. Your cookie choices

When you first visit this website, you are given a choice about whether to allow analytics cookies.

You can:

  • accept analytics cookies;
  • reject analytics cookies; or
  • change your preference later using the Cookie settings link in the website footer.

Rejecting analytics cookies will not prevent you from using the website.

Unless you actively choose to accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics will not be activated through our consent mechanism.

7. Changing or withdrawing your consent

You can change your cookie preference at any time by selecting Cookie settings in the footer of the website.

If you withdraw your consent, we will stop using analytics cookies on your device for future visits, subject to the operation of your browser and the technologies involved.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing that took place before consent was withdrawn.

You can also delete cookies that have already been stored on your device through your browser settings.

8. Browser controls

Most web browsers allow you to view, manage, block or delete cookies.

The way you do this varies between browsers and devices. Please refer to your browser's help or privacy settings for further information.

Blocking all cookies may affect the operation of some websites, although rejecting the optional analytics cookies used on this website will not affect your ability to access its content.

9. Third-party services

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Where you consent to analytics, information collected through Google Analytics may be processed by Google as our service provider.

Google may process information using infrastructure located outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, applicable safeguards are used in accordance with data protection law.

Further information about how we handle personal information and international transfers is available in our Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, including where:

  • the cookies or technologies used on the website change;
  • we change our analytics provider or configuration; or
  • applicable legal or regulatory requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page together with the date on which it was last updated.
