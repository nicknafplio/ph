Privacy Policy

Pigeon Hill Advisory Limited respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it and the rights you have in relation to your personal data.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide to us

If you contact us through the contact form on this website, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the content of your message; and
  • the date and time of your submission.

If you contact us directly by email or through another form of correspondence, we may also retain the information contained in that correspondence.

Website and analytics information

If you consent to analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to collect information about how visitors use the website. This may include information such as:

  • pages viewed;
  • approximate geographic location;
  • device and browser type;
  • referring website or source; and
  • general information about how visitors interact with the website.

Google Analytics does not provide us with information that directly identifies you.

2. How we use your information

We use personal information submitted through this website to:

  • respond to enquiries and correspondence;
  • communicate with you where necessary;
  • consider or take steps in relation to a potential business relationship;
  • maintain appropriate records of our correspondence;
  • operate, maintain and protect the website; and
  • where you have consented to analytics cookies, understand how the website is used and improve its performance and content.

We do not use information submitted through the contact form for marketing purposes unless you have separately agreed to receive such communications.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Our lawful basis for processing

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal information.

Where you contact us with an enquiry, we normally process your information because it is in our legitimate interests to respond to correspondence and manage enquiries relating to our business.

Where your enquiry relates to services you may wish to engage us to provide, processing may also be necessary in order to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Where we use Google Analytics or other optional analytics technologies, we rely on your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time through the website's cookie preferences.

4. How long we keep your information

We retain correspondence and information submitted through the website only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

As a general rule, enquiries that do not result in an ongoing business relationship will be retained for no longer than 24 months from the last meaningful contact.

Information may be retained for longer where this is necessary because of an ongoing business relationship, to establish or defend legal claims, to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation, or for legitimate record-keeping purposes.

When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised.

5. Sharing your information

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help us operate this website and communicate with you. These may include providers of:

  • website hosting and infrastructure;
  • email services;
  • website development and technical support; and
  • website analytics.

These organisations may process personal information on our behalf only where necessary to provide their services and subject to appropriate data protection and confidentiality arrangements.

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation or a court or regulatory authority, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

6. International transfers

Some of the service providers we use may process information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred to a country that does not benefit from an applicable UK adequacy decision, we take appropriate steps to ensure that the information remains protected in accordance with UK data protection law. This may include the use of approved contractual safeguards or another legally recognised transfer mechanism.

You may contact us if you would like further information about the safeguards used in relation to international transfers.

7. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use this website and to improve its content and performance.

Google Analytics is provided by Google. It may collect information about your use of the website, including pages visited, approximate location, device information and how you arrived at the website.

For visitors in the United Kingdom, Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses. IP addresses may be used temporarily to derive general location information before being discarded.

Google Analytics will only operate on this website where you have consented to analytics cookies.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the website's cookie preferences.

8. Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies where necessary for the operation of the site and, with your consent, for analytics.

Optional analytics cookies are not placed on your device unless you choose to accept them.

For more information about the cookies we use and how you can control them, please see our Cookie Policy.

9. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you the right to:

  • request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete your personal information;
  • ask us to restrict the way we use your information;
  • object to certain processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • request the transfer of certain personal information to you or another organisation where the right to data portability applies.

These rights are not absolute and may not apply in every circumstance.

You will not normally be required to pay a fee to exercise your rights.

To make a request, please contact us at talkto@pigeonhilladvisory.co.uk.

10. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we use your personal information, we would encourage you to contact us first so that we can try to resolve the matter.

You also have the right to make a complaint to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's independent data protection regulator.

Further information is available at the ICO website at ico.org.uk.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no transmission of information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Links to other websites

This website may contain links to websites operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the relevant privacy policy when visiting another website.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including where our website, services or legal obligations change.

The latest version will always be published on this page, together with the date on which it was last updated.
