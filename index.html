Pigeon Hill Advisory is an independent adviser to individuals, families and family offices. The role is to bring judgement, perspective and the right specialist connections to matters that rarely have a single obvious answer.

There are no products to promote, no institutional interests to serve, and no house view to defend. Advice is given for one reason only: because it is right for the family or the individual concerned.

Clarity when the path is not obvious.

Introducing Michael Shelton-Agar

Michael founded Pigeon Hill Advisory to offer the kind of counsel he valued most in his own career: independent, direct and unhurried.

His role is to listen closely, ask the questions that matter, and bring the right people together — quietly, and only when it serves the family or the matter at hand.
